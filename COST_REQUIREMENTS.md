## Cost Optimization Tips

1. **Start with Free Tiers**: Most services offer free tiers for startups
2. **Use Serverless**: Reduce costs with serverless functions (AWS Lambda, Vercel)
3. **Optimize Database**: Use connection pooling, caching (Redis)
4. **CDN Caching**: Reduce bandwidth costs with CDN
5. **Monitor Usage**: Set up alerts to avoid unexpected costs
6. **Reserved Instances**: For predictable workloads, use reserved instances (30-50% savings)
7. **Auto-scaling**: Scale down during low-traffic periods
8. **Database Optimization**: Regular cleanup, archiving old data
